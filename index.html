CourtPlay

CourtPlay helps tennis players in Westport, Connecticut fill an open spot in a game. When someone drops out, you post the spot with the date, court, time and skill level, and nearby players can claim it. If you need a game, you can browse open spots and claim one.

How CourtPlay works

  1. Post an open spot. Someone drops out of your game. Post the date, court, time and skill level in about thirty seconds.
  2. Players nearby see it. Everyone in Westport at the right NTRP level gets it in their feed, and by push if they want it.
  3. Someone claims it. You approve the claim, swap details, and play. No group texts, no chasing people.

Sign in with Google to post a spot or claim one. CourtPlay uses your Google account for sign-in only.
